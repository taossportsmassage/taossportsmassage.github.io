About Michael

Michael Larson, LMT

New Mexico License MT-2026-0149

I became a licensed massage therapist in 1997, but my journey started even earlier. As a kid, my family would pay me twenty-five cents to rub their shoulders for fifteen minutes. I did not know it then, but those moments planted the seed for a career dedicated to helping people feel better.

Years later, while studying double bass at Mount Hood Community College, I developed tendonitis in my forearms. A friend arranged for me to receive my first professional massage. That experience changed the direction of my life. It showed me how skilled manual therapy could help someone return to doing what they love.

More than twenty-five years later, I still find the greatest satisfaction in helping people recover from injury, restore healthy movement, and return to the activities that give their lives purpose and joy.

My mission is to help people recover from injury, restore healthy movement, and return to the activities that bring purpose, health, and joy to their lives.
